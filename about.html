Our Story & Clinical Philosophy

Committed to Quality, Ethics, and Gentle Dentistry Since 2005

Over the past 15+ years, our practice has expanded into an accredited multi-specialty dental hospital. Sterilization & Infection Control Protocols

Your Safety and Hygiene is Our Foremost Priority

The Smile Hub strictly complies with international infection control guidelines established by the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Centers for Disease Control (CDC). We operate a rigorous 4-tier sterilization protocol:

Our 4-Step Decontamination Process:

  • Step 1 – Ultrasonic Cleaning: Instruments undergo automated enzymatic ultrasonic bath cleaning to remove biological debris.
  • Step 2 – Medical Packaging: Cleaned instruments are sealed in individual hospital-grade sterilization pouches with integrated chemical indicator strips.
  • Step 3 – Class-B Vacuum Autoclaving: Pouched kits are processed under pressurized steam at 134°C in our computer-monitored Class-B autoclave to eliminate 100% of bacterial spores and viruses.
  • Step 4 – Sterile Storage & Chair-side Opening: Each instrument pouch is opened only in the presence of the patient immediately prior to treatment.

Diagnostic & Treatment Technology

State-of-the-Art Dental Care Equipment

We continuously invest in proven dental technology to make diagnoses faster, procedures more accurate, and visits less time-consuming:

Digital RVG & OPG: Reduces patient radiation exposure by over 80% compared to traditional dental X-rays while providing instantaneous high-resolution digital imaging.
Rotary Endodontic Motors: Precision nickel-titanium motorized files that cleanse curved root canals smoothly and swiftly, allowing single-visit root canal completions.
Intra-Oral Cameras: Tiny high-magnification wand cameras that allow patients to see their teeth and gums in crisp real-time on our chairside color monitors.
Ultrasonic Scalers: Cooled micro-vibration tips that gently dissolve stubborn dental tartar without scraping or weakening natural tooth enamel.
