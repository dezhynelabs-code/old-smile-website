Common Inquiries Regarding Dental Care

Click any question below to expand or collapse the medical explanation

Below are detailed answers to the most frequent inquiries our patients have regarding treatments, procedural comfort, insurance, and recovery:

► Is dental treatment at The Smile Hub painful?

No. We specialize in gentle dentistry and use modern local anesthesia techniques along with computer-controlled dispensers to ensure our patients experience minimal or zero discomfort throughout every procedure, including root canals and tooth extractions.

► How long do professional teeth whitening results last?

With good daily brushing habits, routine flossing, and avoiding excessive consumption of dark tea, coffee, or tobacco, professional in-office whitening results typically remain bright for 12 to 24 months. We also provide custom take-home touch-up trays for periodic maintenance.

► Who is an ideal candidate for dental implants?

Anyone in good general health with healthy gums and adequate jawbone density to support the implant is a candidate. Even with reduced bone mass due to long-term tooth loss, modern bone grafting techniques often allow successful, permanent implant placement.

► How many sittings does a Root Canal Treatment require?

Thanks to our computerized rotary endodontic equipment and digital OPG imaging, over 80% of our root canal treatments are completed in a single 45-to-60 minute sitting. In cases with acute periapical infection or complex root curvature, a second sitting is scheduled to ensure complete sterility before crown fitting.

► What is the difference between metal and ceramic braces?

Traditional metal braces utilize high-grade surgical stainless steel brackets. Ceramic braces use tooth-colored or clear monocrystalline porcelain brackets that blend naturally with your tooth shade, making them far less noticeable in everyday conversation, while offering the same corrective effectiveness.

► Do you accept dental health insurance or cashless mediclaim?

Yes. While routine dental cosmetic care is often excluded by basic Indian mediclaim policies, dental surgeries, accidental trauma repairs, cyst extractions, and hospitalized oral treatments are covered by major corporate TPAs. We provide itemized receipts, doctor clinical summaries, and claim pre-authorization assistance.

► What should I do if a tooth gets knocked out or broken in an accident?

Keep calm! Find the knocked-out tooth immediately. Handle it only by the white crown — never touch the sensitive root tissues. Gently rinse off dirt in cold milk or saline (do not scrub). Place the tooth back into its socket if possible, or keep it in a small cup of cold milk, and visit our emergency dental clinic within 60 minutes for the highest chance of re-implantation.

► How often should I get my teeth professionally cleaned?

The Indian Dental Association and American Dental Association recommend a professional ultrasonic cleaning and checkup every 6 months. For patients with a history of gingivitis, tobacco use, or diabetes, cleanings every 3 to 4 months are recommended to prevent irreversible bone loss.
