DDS • General & Cosmetic Dentistry, Endodontics
10+ Years Clinical Experience

Consultation Hours: Monday – Friday (9:30 AM – 2:00 PM • 4:30 PM – 7:30 PM)

Dr. Arjun Kumar

DMD, MS • Board-Certified Orthodontist & Dentofacial Orthopedist
8+ Years Clinical Experience

Dr. Arjun Kumar earned his DMD from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and completed his orthodontic specialty residency at the University of Michigan. He has successfully treated over 1,200 cases of complex malocclusions using self-ligating metal brackets, tooth-colored clear ceramic braces, and computer-guided clear aligners.

Consultation Hours: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday (10:00 AM – 6:00 PM)

Dr. Meera Raj

DDS, FICOI • Dental Implantologist & Periodontist
7+ Years Clinical Experience

A distinguished Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI, USA), Dr. Meera Raj earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from NYU College of Dentistry. She specializes in surgical implant placement, immediate tooth replacements, bone grafting, and laser-assisted periodontal gum surgeries.
